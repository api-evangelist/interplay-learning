Last week, I heard the story of a Midwestern trade school whose HVAC program is shutting down due to the lack of instructors. There’s no shortage of student interest. More people want skilled trades careers than today’s training programs can prepare, but one of the scarcest resources in those programs is a qualified instructor’s time.
